About me.

I believe the best digital experiences feel simple — because someone cared enough to make them that way.

I'm Mina, a designer and developer who loves bringing people, ideas, and technology together. Over the past six years, I've helped teams turn tangled problems into thoughtful products that people actually enjoy using.

When I'm away from my screen, you'll find me sketching in a café, collecting books I mean to read, or taking the long way home.
